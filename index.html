Areo Conform · Senior aerospace engineer · EASA specialist

Senior Aerospace Drive Systems Design Engineer with practical experience in testing and certification of critical castings made from advanced materials, including EV31A (AMS4429). The work is informed by technical consultations with EASA specialists and international on-site supplier audits at major foundries.

Created to pass on real-world insight, Areo Conform helps junior engineers develop the working understanding required to navigate aerospace certification with more confidence.
